────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. TITLE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HORROR ATMOSPHERE & ENVIRONMENT
Category: Ambient World-State Design
Compatible Engines: Sora, Veo, Kling, Runway, Pika, Luma, Hailuo (video
generation) + GPT/Claude/Gemini/Grok/DeepSeek (atmosphere reasoning)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. PURPOSE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Defines the AMBIENT WORLD-STATE surrounding a scene — weather, air quality,
temperature-implied visual cues, seasonal decay, and environmental motion
(wind, dust, insect activity) — as a distinct layer from location
architecture (Template 05) and lighting (Template 09). Atmosphere is what
makes a location feel ALIVE and PRESENT rather than like a static backdrop;
this template ensures that "liveness" consistently serves dread rather than
being generic scene-dressing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. PROMPT FORMULA
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
[SEASON + WEATHER STATE] + [AIR QUALITY/PARTICULATE DETAIL] +
[TEMPERATURE-IMPLIED VISUAL CUES] + [ENVIRONMENTAL MOTION LAYER] +
[BIOLOGICAL/DECAY PRESENCE] + [AMBIENT WRONGNESS SIGNAL] + [CONSISTENCY
LOCK TOKEN] + [PLATFORM-SPECIFIC SYNTAX WRAPPER]

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. VARIABLES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
{SEASON_AND_WEATHER} — specific season and weather state (not generic
  "stormy night" — specify intensity, duration implied, recent history:
  "third day of unseasonal fog that hasn't lifted")
{AIR_QUALITY_PARTICULATE} — visible atmospheric detail (dust motes in
  light beams, fog density and behavior, smoke, humidity haze, pollen)
{TEMPERATURE_VISUAL_CUES} — how implied temperature reads visually (visible
  breath, condensation on surfaces, frost patterns, heat shimmer) since
  video generation cannot convey temperature directly except through such
  cues
{ENVIRONMENTAL_MOTION} — specific ambient movement (wind direction and
  strength affecting specific objects, curtain movement, tree behavior,
  water surface state) — described precisely enough to remain consistent
{BIOLOGICAL_DECAY_PRESENCE} — organic detail appropriate to setting/story
  (mold growth stage, insect activity type and density, plant overgrowth
  or die-off, animal presence/absence as a wrongness signal)
{AMBIENT_WRONGNESS_SIGNAL} — one specific atmospheric detail that violates
  expected natural behavior (wind that doesn't match visible tree motion,
  fog that moves against the wind, complete absence of insects/birds where
  expected)
{TIME_ELAPSED_INDICATORS} — atmospheric details that convey how much time
  has passed since a key story event (dust accumulation, decay
  progression, weather pattern duration)
{CONSISTENCY_LOCK_TOKEN} — reusable phrase for this atmospheric state,
  scoped to a specific scene-type or story period
{NEGATIVE_TRAITS} — exclude generic/overused atmosphere defaults (no
  generic "spooky fog everywhere" without motivation, no random unmotivated
  wind gusts, no decorative-only cobwebs disconnected from decay logic)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Design the ambient atmospheric world-state for a horror scene/sequence.

SEASON/WEATHER: {SEASON_AND_WEATHER}
AIR QUALITY/PARTICULATE: {AIR_QUALITY_PARTICULATE}
TEMPERATURE VISUAL CUES: {TEMPERATURE_VISUAL_CUES}
ENVIRONMENTAL MOTION: {ENVIRONMENTAL_MOTION}
BIOLOGICAL/DECAY PRESENCE: {BIOLOGICAL_DECAY_PRESENCE}
AMBIENT WRONGNESS SIGNAL: {AMBIENT_WRONGNESS_SIGNAL}
TIME ELAPSED INDICATORS: {TIME_ELAPSED_INDICATORS}
EXCLUDE: {NEGATIVE_TRAITS}

Generate a CONSISTENCY LOCK TOKEN for this atmospheric state and specify
how it should evolve (if at all) across the scenes/sequence it applies to.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. OUTPUT STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. ATMOSPHERE BIBLE SUMMARY (paragraph describing the ambient world-state
   and its intended psychological function)
2. CONSISTENCY LOCK TOKEN (isolated, bolded)
3. SCENE-TYPE VARIANTS (how atmosphere should shift between normal-world
   scenes and wrongness/tension scenes, if applicable)
4. PLATFORM ADAPTATIONS (image-model / video-model natural-language / LLM
   reasoning syntax)
5. CONTINUITY CHECKLIST (fixed atmospheric facts: wind direction, fog
   density, decay stage — must remain constant or evolve only per
   TIME_ELAPSED_INDICATORS)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. PROFESSIONAL INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Atmosphere should always be traceable to a specific, motivated cause
  (real weather patterns, real decay timelines, real biological behavior)
  even when the story is supernatural — grounded atmospheric logic makes
  supernatural elements land harder by contrast.
- Use AMBIENT_WRONGNESS_SIGNAL sparingly and with precision — one well-
  chosen physically-impossible detail (fog moving against the wind) is far
  more effective than many small inconsistencies, which read as generation
  error rather than deliberate design.
- Temperature cannot be shown directly in visual media — always translate
  it into a specific visual cue (breath, condensation, frost) rather than
  leaving it as an unstated environmental fact.
- Track TIME_ELAPSED_INDICATORS carefully across a story's timeline —
  atmospheric decay/growth details are one of the most common continuity
  errors in AI-assisted horror production when scenes are generated out of
  sequence.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. BEST PRACTICES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Establish a baseline atmospheric "normal" for the story's setting early,
  so that any deviation (a sudden windless stillness, an unseasonal
  temperature drop) reads clearly as a deliberate wrongness signal rather
  than random variation.
- Keep environmental motion internally consistent across a single scene —
  if wind direction is established as blowing curtains one way, every
  other wind-affected element in the same scene should match.
- Reserve the most extreme atmospheric wrongness signal for the story's
  most significant moment; don't spend it on an early or minor scene.
- When batching multiple scenes for generation, group by atmospheric state
  where possible to reduce the risk of unintended environmental drift
  between visually adjacent scenes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. HORROR CINEMATIC TECHNIQUES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- THE UNNATURAL STILLNESS: remove expected environmental motion entirely
  (no wind, no insect sound, no bird activity) in scenes preceding a major
  reveal — absence of ambient life is one of horror's most efficient dread
  tools.
- WEATHER AS EMOTIONAL MIRROR (used sparingly): let atmospheric conditions
  track the protagonist's psychological state, but avoid overuse — reserve
  for 1-2 key moments rather than constant pathetic fallacy.
- DECAY AS CLOCK: use progressive biological decay across a location as a
  silent, readable timer for how long since a key event occurred.
- THE WRONG DIRECTION: have fog, smoke, or dust move in a direction that
  contradicts visible wind cues on other objects in frame — small enough
  that it registers as unease before it registers as impossible.
- PARTICULATE AS REVEAL TOOL: use dust motes or fog density to partially
  obscure a creature/threat reveal (Template 04's REVEAL_FRAGMENT_MAP),
  letting atmosphere do reveal-pacing work that lighting alone cannot.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. CAMERA & LIGHTING GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Particulate detail (dust, fog, humidity haze) interacts directly with
  Template 09's light sources — specify how the established LIGHT_SOURCE
  should interact with atmosphere (a single bulb's light cutting visibly
  through dust-heavy air, a flashlight beam scattering in fog).
- Match ENVIRONMENTAL_MOTION to Template 07's camera movement choices —
  static camera shots benefit from environmental motion to prevent visual
  staleness; already-dynamic camera movement may need calmer atmosphere to
  avoid overwhelming the frame.
- Consider how atmosphere affects depth perception and shot scale choices
  (heavy fog naturally limits effective wide-shot legibility, favoring
  medium/close compositions).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11. SOUND & ATMOSPHERE GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Environmental motion should have a matching sound signature (wind
  through specific materials, rain on specific surfaces) passed explicitly
  to Template 13 for audio-visual consistency.
- The absence of expected ambient sound (no insects, no birds, no wind
  despite visible triggers) is itself a powerful and inexpensive atmosphere
  tool — flag these silence-as-atmosphere moments explicitly.
- Biological/decay presence often carries subtle sound correlates (settling
  structures, insect activity) that reinforce the visual atmospheric
  design when included in the sound layer.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
12. EXAMPLE INPUT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SEASON_AND_WEATHER: Late autumn, fourth consecutive day of unseasonal dense
  fog that has not lifted even at midday
AIR_QUALITY_PARTICULATE: Fog density heavy enough to reduce visibility to
  roughly 15-20 meters outdoors; fine moisture visible as haze in any
  direct light source
TEMPERATURE_VISUAL_CUES: Visible breath outdoors and in unheated interior
  spaces; condensation beading on the relay vault's cold metal equipment
ENVIRONMENTAL_MOTION: No wind at all for the full fourth day — a stillness
  notably different from the light breeze of the preceding three days
BIOLOGICAL_DECAY_PRESENCE: No birds visible or audible anywhere on the
  property since the fog began; moderate dust accumulation throughout
  consistent with roughly two decades of abandonment
AMBIENT_WRONGNESS_SIGNAL: The fog does not disperse or thin at any point
  during the fourth day, including at midday when autumn fog would
  naturally burn off
TIME_ELAPSED_INDICATORS: Dust accumulation on the logbook and desk
  consistent with the stated ~50 years since abandonment; fog specifically
  began four days ago, correlating with the story's inciting event
NEGATIVE_TRAITS: No generic decorative cobwebs disconnected from the decay
  timeline, no random gusts contradicting the established stillness

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
13. EXAMPLE OUTPUT (Condensed)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ATMOSPHERE BIBLE SUMMARY: A fourth unbroken day of windless, unlifting fog
that should have burned off by midday and hasn't, in a place where the
birds stopped making sound the same day it arrived — the atmosphere itself
is the story's first, quietest clue that something has changed.

CONSISTENCY LOCK TOKEN:
"Fourth day of dense unlifting fog, no wind, no birds, visible breath,
condensation on cold metal, ~15m visibility"

SCENE-TYPE VARIANT — Approaching the Building (Build beat):
[Lock Token] + "the exchange building emerges gradually from the fog at
close range, its outline visibly damp, no birds break the silence, her
breath fogs visibly in the still, windless air"

PLATFORM ADAPTATIONS:
- Video-model syntax: "Dense fog blankets the property, thick enough to
  limit visibility to about fifteen meters, unmoved by any wind on this,
  the fourth straight day it has failed to lift even at midday. No birds
  call anywhere nearby. Her breath is visible in the cold, still air as
  she approaches."

CONTINUITY CHECKLIST: Fog must remain equally dense and unlifting across
every exterior scene set on "day four" ✓. Absence of birds must be
consistent throughout ✓. Any scene set on days 1-3 must show light breeze
and thinner fog for contrast ✓.

14. ADVANCED VERSION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Add for multi-day or seasonal-progression stories:

{MULTI_DAY_ATMOSPHERIC_PROGRESSION} — a day-by-day atmospheric evolution
  chart for stories spanning an extended timeline, ensuring gradual and
  trackable change rather than random daily variation
{CLIMATE_LOGIC_DOCUMENT} — for supernatural stories where atmosphere itself
  is partially controlled by the antagonist, define the exact rules
  governing when/how this control manifests (rules prevent it from feeling
  arbitrary)
{CROSS_LOCATION_ATMOSPHERIC_CONTRAST} — if the story moves between
  distinct locations, define how atmosphere should contrast between them to
  reinforce the isolation vector from Template 01
{PARTICULATE_VFX_HANDOFF} — flag any atmospheric element (heavy fog
  interaction with practical light, complex decay detail) that may need
  Template 14/15 VFX layering rather than being achievable in a single
  generation pass

ADVANCED OUTPUT ADDITION: Request an "ATMOSPHERIC CONTINUITY TIMELINE" — a
simple chronological chart mapping every scene to its correct atmospheric
state per TIME_ELAPSED_INDICATORS, to catch out-of-sequence generation
errors before they compound.
