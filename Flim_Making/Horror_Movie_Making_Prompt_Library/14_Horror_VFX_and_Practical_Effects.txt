────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. TITLE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HORROR VFX & PRACTICAL EFFECTS
Category: Visual & Practical Effects Design
Compatible Engines: Sora, Veo, Kling, Runway, Pika, Luma, Hailuo (for
generated effects) + GPT/Claude/Gemini/Grok/DeepSeek (effects reasoning
and feasibility planning)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. PURPOSE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Designs body horror, transformation, injury, and impossible-physical-
phenomena effects with a specific discipline: deciding, for each effect,
whether it is best achieved through AI generation, practical/physical
techniques, or a hybrid, and specifying the effect with enough technical
precision to be executed either way. This template also governs realistic
injury/gore logic so that effects read as believable rather than
gratuitous or generic.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. PROMPT FORMULA
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
[EFFECT TYPE + STORY PURPOSE] + [ANATOMICAL/PHYSICAL ACCURACY BASELINE] +
[TRANSFORMATION/PROGRESSION STAGES] + [MATERIAL AND TEXTURE SPECIFICATION]
+ [EXECUTION METHOD: AI/PRACTICAL/HYBRID] + [GORE CALIBRATION LEVEL] +
[REVEAL PACING] + [PLATFORM-SPECIFIC SYNTAX WRAPPER]

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. VARIABLES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
{EFFECT_TYPE} — specific effect category (practical injury/wound, body
  horror transformation, impossible physical phenomenon, decay/
  deterioration effect, environmental destruction)
{STORY_PURPOSE} — why this effect exists narratively (never effect for
  its own sake — tie back to Template 01's thematic spine or antagonist
  logic)
{ANATOMICAL_PHYSICAL_BASELINE} — the realistic physical/anatomical starting
  point being altered, described accurately before deviation is applied
{TRANSFORMATION_STAGES} — if progressive, 3-5 numbered stages from initial
  to final state, each independently specified
{MATERIAL_TEXTURE_SPEC} — precise material qualities (viscosity, color
  progression, light interaction, practical material equivalents like
  silicone/gelatin analogs for reference)
{EXECUTION_METHOD} — AI-generation-only / practical-effects-only / hybrid
  (AI-generated base with practical compositing, or vice versa), with
  reasoning for the choice
{GORE_CALIBRATION} — 1-10 explicit scale, cross-referenced to
  {RATING_TARGET} from Template 02, with a preference toward implication
  and aftermath over explicit graphic display unless the story specifically
  calls for higher calibration
{REVEAL_PACING} — how much of the effect is shown directly vs. implied
  through reaction/aftermath/partial view
{CONTINUITY_STATE} — how this effect should appear consistently in any
  subsequent scenes (a wound doesn't heal or change inconsistently)
{NEGATIVE_TRAITS} — exclude generic/unrealistic defaults (no unmotivated
  excessive gore disconnected from story purpose, no physically implausible
  effects without an established supernatural justification, no effect
  that exists purely for shock without narrative function)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. INPUT STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Design a horror VFX/practical effect with realistic grounding and clear
execution planning.

EFFECT TYPE: {EFFECT_TYPE}
STORY PURPOSE: {STORY_PURPOSE}
ANATOMICAL/PHYSICAL BASELINE: {ANATOMICAL_PHYSICAL_BASELINE}
TRANSFORMATION STAGES (if applicable): {TRANSFORMATION_STAGES}
MATERIAL/TEXTURE SPEC: {MATERIAL_TEXTURE_SPEC}
EXECUTION METHOD: {EXECUTION_METHOD}
GORE CALIBRATION (1-10): {GORE_CALIBRATION}
REVEAL PACING: {REVEAL_PACING}
CONTINUITY STATE: {CONTINUITY_STATE}
EXCLUDE: {NEGATIVE_TRAITS}

Specify the effect with enough technical precision that it could be
executed either via AI generation or practical effects reference,
according to EXECUTION_METHOD, and confirm the effect serves STORY_PURPOSE
rather than existing as gratuitous spectacle.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. OUTPUT STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. EFFECT DESIGN SUMMARY (paragraph: what the effect is, why it serves the
   story, and its intended audience impact)
2. STAGE-BY-STAGE SPECIFICATION (if progressive, one detailed description
   per transformation stage)
3. PLATFORM-READY GENERATION PROMPT (natural-language, video-model-
   optimized description for AI generation)
4. PRACTICAL EFFECTS REFERENCE NOTE (translatable guidance for physical
   effects execution, useful for hybrid productions)
5. CONTINUITY CHECKLIST (fixed facts about the effect's appearance that
   must remain consistent in all subsequent scenes)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. PROFESSIONAL INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Always establish ANATOMICAL_PHYSICAL_BASELINE accurately before
  describing deviation — effects that violate real anatomy convincingly
  require first understanding what "convincing" means for that anatomy;
  effects built on inaccurate baselines read as fake regardless of
  technical polish.
- Favor implication and aftermath over sustained explicit display for most
  story purposes — a brief, well-placed glimpse combined with strong
  reaction performance (Template 11) is frequently more effective and more
  reusable across platforms with content restrictions than sustained
  graphic detail.
- Every effect should trace to STORY_PURPOSE — if an effect can be removed
  without weakening the narrative or theme, reconsider whether it belongs
  in the project at this calibration level.
- Note that most current AI video generation platforms have content
  policies restricting graphic gore/injury content; keep GORE_CALIBRATION
  reasonable for the target platform and lean on Template 07/09's
  suggestion-through-shadow-and-editing techniques where direct generation
  isn't permitted or advisable.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. BEST PRACTICES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Design transformation effects in discrete, independently-specified
  stages rather than one continuous vague description — this both improves
  AI generation consistency and gives editors precise control over reveal
  pacing.
- Cross-check GORE_CALIBRATION against the project's overall RATING_TARGET
  (Template 02) before finalizing any individual effect, so the film's
  tone remains consistent rather than having isolated scenes that spike
  far above or below the established calibration.
- When AI platform content restrictions limit direct generation of an
  effect, default to Template 07's camera techniques (partial framing,
  reaction shots, careful editing implication) rather than attempting to
  circumvent platform policies.
- Maintain a single continuity document per character/effect tracking
  exactly how injuries or transformations should appear at every point in
  the story, to prevent inconsistent healing or progression across
  scenes generated out of order.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. HORROR CINEMATIC TECHNIQUES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- AFTERMATH OVER ACTION: show the result of a violent/transformative event
  rather than the event itself, letting the audience's imagination
  construct what happened — frequently more disturbing and more broadly
  usable than direct depiction.
- THE PARTIAL GLIMPSE: reveal an effect through a brief, partially obscured
  view (reflection, peripheral framing, quick cut) rather than a sustained
  clear shot, preserving mystery and reducing content-policy friction
  simultaneously.
- REACTION AS EFFECT: let a witnessing character's physical/vocal reaction
  (Template 11) carry the emotional weight of an effect the camera never
  directly shows.
- PROGRESSIVE WRONGNESS: for transformation effects, make early stages
  subtle enough to be nearly missed, so the audience's realization builds
  cumulatively rather than arriving all at once.
- TEXTURE OVER GRAPHIC DETAIL: emphasize unsettling texture and material
  quality (wetness, unnatural color, wrongness of surface) over explicit
  anatomical detail, which is often more effective and more broadly
  platform-compatible.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. CAMERA & LIGHTING GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Pair REVEAL_PACING decisions directly with Template 07's shot scale and
  Template 09's shadow architecture — effects intended for partial reveal
  should be explicitly matched with concealing lighting/framing rather
  than left to chance.
- Use Template 07's rack-focus or partial-occlusion framing techniques to
  control exactly how much of an effect is legible at any given moment.
- Consider that harsh, direct lighting (Template 09) reveals texture and
  detail with maximum clarity — reserve this lighting choice specifically
  for effects intended for full clear reveal, and use softer/dimmer/more
  shadow-heavy lighting for effects intended to stay ambiguous.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11. SOUND & ATMOSPHERE GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Define a specific sound signature for the effect (a transformation's
  particular sound, an injury's specific audio quality) to pass to
  Template 13 — sound often carries more of an effect's visceral impact
  than the visual itself, especially when visual detail is intentionally
  limited.
- Consider using sound to imply an effect's severity beyond what's shown
  visually, allowing lower GORE_CALIBRATION visual choices to still land
  with full narrative impact through audio design.
- Match effect-related sound timing precisely to REVEAL_PACING stages for
  synchronized audio-visual impact.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
12. EXAMPLE INPUT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
EFFECT_TYPE: Body horror transformation (a minor character slowly
  "becoming" part of the commune's collective in the film's climax)
STORY_PURPOSE: Visualizes the thematic spine (belonging bought through
  self-erasure) literally — the character's individual features gradually
  homogenize toward a shared, communal appearance
ANATOMICAL_PHYSICAL_BASELINE: A specific named secondary character with
  established, distinct facial features (from Template 03)
TRANSFORMATION_STAGES: 1) Baseline — fully distinct individual appearance
  2) Subtle — skin tone shifts marginally toward the weathered tone shared
  by long-term commune members 3) Moderate — one distinctive individual
  feature (an asymmetric feature) begins to soften toward symmetry
  4) Advanced — expression defaults to the same neutral half-smile shared
  by other transformed members 5) Complete — visually indistinguishable at
  a glance from other long-term members, individual identity erased
MATERIAL_TEXTURE_SPEC: No overt "monster" texture — the horror is in
  increasing NORMALCY and homogeneity, not grotesque deviation; skin
  should remain human-realistic throughout, only trending toward
  sameness
EXECUTION_METHOD: AI-generation primary, achieved through incremental
  prompt adjustment across the 5 stages using consistent character tokens
  with deliberate, small parameter shifts
GORE_CALIBRATION: 1/10 — this effect is explicitly bloodless; its horror
  is conceptual/visual homogenization, not violence
REVEAL_PACING: Gradual across multiple scenes spanning the film's back
  half; never called attention to explicitly, discovered only if the
  audience compares early and late appearances
CONTINUITY_STATE: Once a stage is reached, the character never reverts to
  an earlier stage for the remainder of the story
NEGATIVE_TRAITS: No grotesque body horror imagery, no visible pain/gore, no
  overt "monster" transformation — the horror is the loss of individual
  distinctness itself

13. EXAMPLE OUTPUT (Condensed)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
EFFECT DESIGN SUMMARY: This transformation carries no blood and no overt
monstrousness — its horror is the slow erasure of a specific, distinct
face into interchangeable sameness, visualizing the film's thematic core
without a single drop of visual gore.

STAGE-BY-STAGE SPECIFICATION:
Stage 1 (Baseline): [Character's established Template 03 lock token, used
  verbatim, no modification]
Stage 3 (Moderate — example mid-point): "[Character lock token, modified]:
  skin tone shifted subtly toward a weathered, sun-worn quality shared by
  long-term commune members; the character's signature asymmetric smile
  now noticeably softer, trending toward the same even half-smile seen in
  other transformed figures"
Stage 5 (Complete): "A commune member whose individual features have
  become difficult to distinguish from others around them — same
  weathered tone, same even half-smile, same unhurried stillness — visible
  only as the same figure introduced [X scenes] earlier if directly
  compared"

PLATFORM-READY GENERATION PROMPT (Stage 3 example): "The character stands
among other commune members at dusk. Her skin carries a new, weathered
warmth it didn't have before. Her once-distinctive smile now sits more
evenly across her face, softer, less her own."

PRACTICAL EFFECTS REFERENCE NOTE: If achieved practically, this would rely
on subtle makeup tone-matching and directed performance adjustment (a
performer note to soften a previously distinctive expression) rather than
prosthetics — the "effect" is primarily a performance and continuity
choice, not a physical appliance.

CONTINUITY CHECKLIST: Once Stage 3 is reached in a given scene, no
subsequent earlier-numbered scene should show the character at Stage 1 or
2 ✓. Progression is one-directional only ✓.
