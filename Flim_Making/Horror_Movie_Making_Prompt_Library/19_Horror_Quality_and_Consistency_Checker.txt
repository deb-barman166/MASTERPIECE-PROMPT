1. TITLE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HORROR QUALITY & CONSISTENCY CHECKER
Category: Diagnostic Review & Continuity Audit
Compatible Engines: GPT-4/5, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, and future LLMs
(used as a reasoning/review layer over generated text and visual outputs)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. PURPOSE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Provides a systematic AUDIT PROTOCOL to run against completed or in-
progress generations from any other template in this library, checking for
continuity breaks, consistency-token drift, tonal inconsistency, and
craft-level quality issues before content moves further down the
production pipeline. This template is the library's quality-control
backstop — it doesn't generate new content, it verifies that what's been
generated actually honors the locked decisions made in every prior
template.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. PROMPT FORMULA
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
[CONTENT UNDER REVIEW] + [RELEVANT LOCKED TOKENS/BIBLES FOR COMPARISON] +
[AUDIT CATEGORY SELECTION] + [SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM] +
[CORRECTIVE RECOMMENDATION FORMAT] + [RE-VERIFICATION PROTOCOL]

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. VARIABLES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
{CONTENT_UNDER_REVIEW} — the generated scene, dialogue, character
  description, or sequence to be audited
{RELEVANT_LOCKED_TOKENS} — all applicable consistency lock tokens and
  bibles from prior templates (character, creature, location,
  cinematography, lighting) that this content must honor

{AUDIT_CATEGORY} — select relevant categories per review:
  - VISUAL CONSISTENCY: does character/creature/location appearance match
    locked tokens exactly (Templates 03/04/05)
  - TONAL CONSISTENCY: does this content match the established
    PRACTICAL_VS_STYLIZED_BALANCE and overall tone calibration (Template
    08)
  - NARRATIVE CONTINUITY: does this content contradict established plot
    facts, character knowledge, or timeline (Template 01/02)
  - ANTAGONIST LOGIC INTEGRITY: does the antagonist/creature behave within
    its established rules (Template 01/04's ANTAGONIST_LOGIC)
  - PACING INTEGRITY: does this content honor the established
    BEAT_TYPE/TENSION_CURVE rather than flattening or rushing it (Template
    02/06)
  - CLICHÉ/GENERIC-DEFAULT DETECTION: has this content drifted toward
    generic horror tropes despite the specific design intentions
    established across the library
  - DIALOGUE SUBTEXT INTEGRITY: does dialogue maintain the withholding
    discipline established in Template 12, or has it become on-the-nose
  - EFFECTS CONTINUITY: do injury/transformation states match their
    established progression stage (Template 14)

{SEVERITY_CLASSIFICATION} — CRITICAL (breaks story logic or core design,
  must be fixed before proceeding) / MODERATE (noticeable inconsistency,
  should be fixed) / MINOR (small polish issue, fix if time permits)
{CORRECTIVE_RECOMMENDATION_FORMAT} — for each flagged issue, a specific,
  actionable fix rather than just a problem description
{RE_VERIFICATION_PROTOCOL} — instructions for confirming a fix actually
  resolved the flagged issue without introducing new inconsistencies

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. INPUT STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Audit this content against established project consistency requirements.

CONTENT UNDER REVIEW: {CONTENT_UNDER_REVIEW}
RELEVANT LOCKED TOKENS/BIBLES: {RELEVANT_LOCKED_TOKENS}
AUDIT CATEGORIES TO CHECK: {AUDIT_CATEGORY}

For each selected audit category, systematically compare the content under
review against the relevant locked tokens/bibles and story facts,
identifying any discrepancy, however small. Classify each finding by
severity and provide a specific corrective recommendation.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. OUTPUT STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. AUDIT SUMMARY (overall pass/fail assessment with total issue count by
   severity)
2. DETAILED FINDINGS TABLE (category | specific issue | severity |
   corrective recommendation)
3. CRITICAL ISSUES REQUIRING IMMEDIATE ATTENTION (isolated and highlighted
   separately from the full table for quick triage)
4. RE-VERIFICATION CHECKLIST (specific points to re-check after
   corrections are applied)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. PROFESSIONAL INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Compare against LOCKED TOKENS LITERALLY — don't assume minor paraphrasing
  drift is acceptable; if a character's established eye color, a
  creature's established limb count, or a location's established
  architectural feature has changed even slightly, flag it.
- Distinguish between DELIBERATE, STORY-MOTIVATED changes (a character's
  wardrobe degrading per an established arc) and UNINTENDED DRIFT (a
  character's face shape subtly changing between generations) — only the
  latter is a genuine consistency failure.
- When auditing antagonist logic integrity, check specifically whether the
  content shows the antagonist violating its own established rules
  (Template 01/04) without narrative justification — rule violations
  without justification are critical-severity issues since they undermine
  the entire fear architecture.
- Always provide a specific, actionable corrective recommendation
  alongside every flagged issue — a quality audit that only identifies
  problems without suggesting fixes creates extra work rather than saving
  it.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. BEST PRACTICES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Run this audit at natural production checkpoints (after each major
  scene, after each act, before final assembly) rather than only at the
  very end, since catching drift early prevents it from compounding
  across many dependent generations.
- Keep a running MASTER CONSISTENCY DOCUMENT compiling every locked token
  and story fact from Templates 01-15, and reference this single document
  during every audit rather than re-deriving facts from scattered prior
  outputs each time.
- When the same type of drift keeps appearing across multiple audits (a
  specific character detail repeatedly rendering incorrectly), address the
  root cause in the originating template's lock token rather than
  continuing to catch and fix it downstream every time.
- Don't over-audit minor stylistic variation that doesn't affect story
  logic or core design — reserve rigorous scrutiny for elements the story
  actually depends on, to keep the audit process efficient and focused.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. HORROR CINEMATIC TECHNIQUES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- THE REVEAL LEAKAGE CHECK: specifically audit every "hint" and "partial"
  stage output from Template 04's reveal architecture to confirm no
  later-stage detail has leaked into an earlier stage's description.
- THE RESTRAINT DRIFT CHECK: specifically audit performance (Template 11)
  and dialogue (Template 12) outputs for creeping melodrama or on-the-nose
  exposition, since these tend to drift toward genre-generic defaults over
  a long production more than other elements.
- THE MOTIF DISCIPLINE CHECK: verify that signature sound/visual motifs
  (Templates 01/04/13) have not been used outside their established
  trigger conditions, which would weaken their conditioning effect on the
  audience.
- THE ESCALATION LADDER CHECK: verify that tension/intensity across
  reviewed scenes actually escalates according to Template 01's
  ESCALATION_BEATS rather than plateauing or peaking prematurely.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. CAMERA & LIGHTING GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Audit camera language (Template 07) specifically for SIGNATURE_MOTIF_
  LOCK violations — confirm the antagonist-reserved camera combination
  hasn't been accidentally reused for unrelated content.
- Audit lighting continuity (Template 09) by checking that established
  per-location light source rules haven't been contradicted without a
  specific story reason (e.g., a power restoration beat).
- Cross-check Template 08's cinematography lock token against a sample of
  generated content periodically to catch gradual color/contrast drift
  before it compounds across many scenes.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11. SOUND & ATMOSPHERE GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Audit sound design outputs (Template 13) against the established
  SILENCE_CUE_PLACEMENT and MOTIF_TOKEN rules, confirming silence hasn't
  been unintentionally filled and the motif hasn't appeared outside its
  established trigger logic.
- Audit atmospheric continuity (Template 10) across a sequence of scenes
  to confirm weather/environmental states progress consistently with
  TIME_ELAPSED_INDICATORS rather than resetting or jumping inconsistently.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
12. EXAMPLE INPUT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CONTENT_UNDER_REVIEW: A newly generated scene showing Warden Voss raising
  his voice and speaking rapidly to another commune member
RELEVANT_LOCKED_TOKENS: Voss's Template 03 character bible, which
  specifies: "unhurried," "measured," "never a raised voice" as explicit
  NEGATIVE_TRAITS
AUDIT_CATEGORIES_TO_CHECK: Visual consistency, Tonal consistency,
  Narrative continuity

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
13. EXAMPLE OUTPUT (Condensed)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
AUDIT SUMMARY: FAIL — 1 critical issue identified. This scene directly
contradicts an explicit, established character constraint.

DETAILED FINDINGS TABLE:
| Category | Issue | Severity | Recommendation |
|----------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Visual/Tonal Consistency | Voss is shown raising his voice and speaking
rapidly, directly contradicting his Template 03 lock token's explicit
NEGATIVE_TRAITS ("no raised voice ever," "moves/speaks with deliberate
slowness") | CRITICAL | Regenerate the scene with Voss maintaining his
established unhurried, measured, quiet register even under dramatic
pressure — if the story requires him to convey urgency, use pacing,
word choice, and the established throat-touch tell (per his character
bible) rather than volume or speed to convey it |

CRITICAL ISSUES REQUIRING IMMEDIATE ATTENTION: The Voss characterization
break above must be corrected before this scene proceeds to video
generation (Template 17), since his unnerving stillness is a core,
load-bearing element of his character design — losing it undermines his
established uncanny effect throughout the entire project, not just this
scene.

RE-VERIFICATION CHECKLIST: After regeneration, confirm — (1) Voss's vocal
volume/pace remains constant regardless of scene stakes, (2) urgency, if
needed, is conveyed through word choice or the throat-touch tell instead,
(3) no other established NEGATIVE_TRAITS from his character bible have
been introduced in the corrected version.
