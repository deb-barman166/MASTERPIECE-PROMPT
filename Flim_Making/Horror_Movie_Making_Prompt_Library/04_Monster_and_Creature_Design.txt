────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. TITLE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MONSTER & CREATURE DESIGN
Category: Non-Human Entity Visual Development
Compatible Engines: Sora, Veo, Kling, Runway, Pika, Luma, Hailuo (visual
generation) + GPT/Claude/Gemini/Grok/DeepSeek (design reasoning)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. PURPOSE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Designs non-human horror entities — monsters, creatures, supernatural forms,
body-horror transformations — with an emphasis on BIOLOGICAL/PHYSICAL LOGIC
and PARTIAL-REVEAL ARCHITECTURE. The template's central discipline is
building a creature that makes sense as a "thing" (it has an origin, a
locomotion logic, a reason for its form) rather than a random assemblage of
scary parts, because internally consistent monsters are what separate
Hollywood-tier design from generic AI-slop creature generation.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. PROMPT FORMULA
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
[ORIGIN LOGIC] + [ANATOMICAL BASE + DEVIATION POINTS] + [LOCOMOTION LOGIC] +
[SCALE + PROPORTION RULES] + [SURFACE/TEXTURE DETAIL] + [REVEAL FRAGMENT
MAP: WHAT SHOWS AT EACH STAGE] + [BEHAVIORAL SIGNATURE] + [CONSISTENCY LOCK
TOKEN] + [PLATFORM-SPECIFIC SYNTAX WRAPPER]

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. VARIABLES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
{ORIGIN_LOGIC} — where this creature came from and why it looks the way it
  does (biological mutation, ritual transformation, alien/cosmic, digital/
  synthetic corruption) — form must follow origin
{ANATOMICAL_BASE} — the recognizable base this deviates from (humanoid,
  quadruped, insectoid, amorphous, architectural/environmental)
{DEVIATION_POINTS} — 2-4 specific anatomical wrongnesses (joint that bends
  backward, too many/too few of something, proportion violation) — fewer,
  well-chosen deviations outperform maximalist "everything is wrong" design
{LOCOMOTION_LOGIC} — exactly how it moves, specifically, and why that
  movement is unsettling (too many points of contact, unnatural speed
  changes, moves like it's being operated rather than living)
{SCALE_PROPORTION} — size relative to human characters, described precisely
{SURFACE_TEXTURE} — skin/exterior detail, with attention to how light and
  shadow interact with it (matte/absorbing vs. wet/reflective changes
  horror readability enormously)
{COLOR_LOGIC} — palette, and whether it's camouflaged-into-environment or
  jarringly present
{REVEAL_FRAGMENT_MAP} — what portion of the creature is visible at each of:
  first hint, first sighting, mid-story partial view, full reveal (if ever)
{BEHAVIORAL_SIGNATURE} — one specific recurring behavior pattern (a sound
  before attacking, a stillness tell, a hunting pattern)
{SENSORY_DETAIL} — smell, sound, temperature associated with proximity
{CONSISTENCY_LOCK_TOKEN} — reusable short phrase for cross-scene consistency
{NEGATIVE_TRAITS} — explicitly exclude generic tropes (no generic zombie
  gait unless subverted, no simple black-eyes-and-fangs default, no
  copyrighted creature resemblance)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. INPUT STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Design a non-human horror entity for image/video generation with internally
consistent biological/physical logic.

ORIGIN LOGIC: {ORIGIN_LOGIC}
ANATOMICAL BASE: {ANATOMICAL_BASE}
DEVIATION POINTS: {DEVIATION_POINTS}
LOCOMOTION LOGIC: {LOCOMOTION_LOGIC}
SCALE: {SCALE_PROPORTION}
SURFACE/TEXTURE: {SURFACE_TEXTURE}
COLOR LOGIC: {COLOR_LOGIC}
BEHAVIORAL SIGNATURE: {BEHAVIORAL_SIGNATURE}
SENSORY DETAIL: {SENSORY_DETAIL}
REVEAL MAP: {REVEAL_FRAGMENT_MAP}
EXCLUDE: {NEGATIVE_TRAITS}

Generate:
1. A CONSISTENCY LOCK TOKEN capturing the unmistakable silhouette/signature
   of the creature in under 25 words.
2. Four fragment-stage prompts, one for each stage in REVEAL_FRAGMENT_MAP,
   each describing ONLY what should be visible at that stage — do not leak
   full-creature detail into early-stage prompts.
3. Platform-specific syntax adaptations.

Constraint: The creature design must justify itself against ORIGIN_LOGIC —
every anatomical deviation should be traceable to the stated origin. Reject
"scary for scary's sake" additions that don't serve the origin logic.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. OUTPUT STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. CREATURE BIBLE SUMMARY (paragraph covering origin, form, and why it's
   frightening beyond surface appearance)
2. CONSISTENCY LOCK TOKEN (isolated, bolded)
3. REVEAL-STAGE PROMPT SET (4 prompts: hint / first sighting / partial /
   full — each self-contained and platform-ready)
4. PLATFORM ADAPTATIONS (image-model / video-model natural-language / LLM
   reasoning-block syntax)
5. CONTINUITY CHECKLIST (fixed facts that must never contradict: number of
   limbs, scale, texture, color)
6. ORIGINALITY VERIFICATION NOTE (a brief statement on how this design
   avoids resembling existing copyrighted creatures/franchises)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
7. PROFESSIONAL INSTRUCTIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Fewer, sharper deviations beat maximalist design. A creature with one
  deeply wrong feature (a joint bending the wrong way) is more effective
  than a creature with twelve random horror features stacked together.
- Locomotion is often scarier than appearance. Specify HOW it moves with as
  much care as WHAT it looks like — many iconic horror creatures are
  defined by movement (unnatural speed, wrong gait) more than static form.
- Texture and light interaction matter more than color. A matte, light-
  absorbing surface reads as a void; a wet, light-catching surface reads as
  organic and recently-alive. Choose deliberately per the intended effect.
- Never fully explain the creature's biology in-world through dialogue —
  this template is for VISUAL design; narrative explanation belongs in
  Template 01, and even there should remain fragmentary.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
8. BEST PRACTICES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Design the silhouette first, then details — most horror creature
  recognition happens through silhouette in low light, so validate the lock
  token reads correctly as a dark shape before adding surface detail.
- Keep the REVEAL_FRAGMENT_MAP strict — writing a "hint" stage prompt that
  accidentally includes full-body detail defeats the entire reveal
  architecture and should be caught before use.
- Cap named deviation points at 2-4; test shows more than this causes most
  current-generation models to blend deviations into visual noise rather
  than distinct, readable wrongness.
- Cross-check design against NEGATIVE_TRAITS for accidental resemblance to
  well-known copyrighted creatures/media before finalizing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
9. HORROR CINEMATIC TECHNIQUES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- SILHOUETTE-FIRST DESIGN: the creature should be identifiable and unsettling
  as a pure black shape against light before any texture is visible.
- THE WRONG NUMBER: a single deviation in count (fingers, joints, eyes) reads
  as far more disturbing than total anatomical replacement.
- MOVEMENT BEFORE FORM: introduce the creature via its motion signature (in
  peripheral vision, in a mirror, in a reflection) before a clear shape is
  ever presented.
- SCALE AMBIGUITY: withhold clear scale reference in early appearances so
  the audience cannot judge size/danger — reveal true scale only at a
  chosen dramatic beat.
- THE STILLNESS TRAP: for many creature types, stillness that mimics an
  inanimate object (a coat rack, a tree, furniture) until sudden movement
  is a highly effective technique — specify if applicable.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
10. CAMERA & LIGHTING GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Specify how each reveal stage should be lit: hints typically favor
  backlight/silhouette, partial reveals favor raking side-light that shows
  texture without full form, full reveals (if used) can afford even
  frontal light since mystery has been spent.
- Note whether the creature should be lit as though it ABSORBS or REFLECTS
  ambient light differently than the environment around it — this single
  choice heavily impacts perceived wrongness.
- Cross-reference Template 07 for exact lens/angle pairing per reveal
  stage — wide lenses distort proportion for unease, telephoto compresses
  for a suffocating, "closer than it should be" feeling.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11. SOUND & ATMOSPHERE GUIDE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Define a signature sound tied to locomotion (not just a generic roar/
  screech) — the specific sound the wrongness makes when it moves is often
  more memorable than any visual.
- Note environmental sound displacement: does the creature's presence
  silence ambient sound (birds stop, wind stops) or add to it? This choice
  should be locked here and passed to Template 13.
- Define a pre-appearance audio tell (distinct from the sound it makes while
  visible) for use in the Threat Ladder technique from Template 01/09.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
12. EXAMPLE INPUT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
ORIGIN_LOGIC: A drowning victim's body reanimated by lakewater that
  "remembers" the shape of everyone who has ever drowned in it, layering
  their forms imperfectly
ANATOMICAL_BASE: Humanoid
DEVIATION_POINTS: Face shifts between at least three overlapping visages
  when viewed directly; skin has the sheen and give of standing water, not
  flesh; joints articulate one extra time each (a "double elbow" motion)
LOCOMOTION_LOGIC: Moves only when not directly observed — freezes mid-motion
  the instant it is looked at directly, like a stopped video frame
SCALE_PROPORTION: Human-sized, but height shifts subtly by up to 6 inches
  between appearances (matching different drowning victims' statures)
SURFACE_TEXTURE: Wet-sheen, rippling faintly, never fully dry, catches light
  like disturbed water
COLOR_LOGIC: Pallid, lake-water grey-green, no natural skin tone
BEHAVIORAL_SIGNATURE: Always found closer to the nearest body of water than
  physically possible given time elapsed since last sighting
SENSORY_DETAIL: Smell of stagnant water and cold; temperature drop of
  several degrees in its presence
REVEAL_FRAGMENT_MAP: Hint = ripples with no source; First sighting = a wet
  handprint drying nowhere near water; Partial = glimpsed still-frame
  silhouette at the edge of vision; Full = only in climax, direct
  confrontation at the water's edge
NEGATIVE_TRAITS: No generic "ghost girl in wet hair" trope, no resemblance
  to existing J-horror franchise antagonists

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
13. EXAMPLE OUTPUT (Condensed)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CREATURE BIBLE SUMMARY: The entity is a lake-memory composite that can only
move unobserved, freezing into an unnervingly specific still frame the
instant it's looked at directly — its horror lies not in its overlapping,
water-sheened face but in the dawning realization that it was moving a
half-second before you turned around.

CONSISTENCY LOCK TOKEN:
"Lake-memory figure, wet grey-green sheen skin, overlapping shifting
visages, freezes to a stopped-frame stillness the instant it's observed
directly"

REVEAL-STAGE PROMPTS:
- HINT: "Concentric ripples spread across still lake water with no visible
  source, the shoreline empty in every direction, dusk light."
- FIRST SIGHTING: "A single wet handprint glistens on a dry wooden dock
  railing, far from the water's edge, slowly darkening the wood beneath
  it as it seeps in."
- PARTIAL: "A humanoid shape stands frozen mid-stride at the edge of a
  hallway, wet grey-green skin catching hallway light like disturbed water,
  visible only in peripheral framing, gone from a direct angle."
- FULL (climax only): "At the water's edge, a human silhouette with skin
  like rippling grey-green water stands motionless, its face resolving into
  three overlapping, shifting visages as it is finally looked at directly."

PLATFORM ADAPTATIONS:
- Video-model syntax: "A figure with wet, grey-green, water-like skin
  stands frozen at the treeline, mid-stride, as if paused. Its face seems
  to hold two or three overlapping expressions at once, shifting subtly.
  The moment the camera holds on it directly, it does not move at all."
- Image-model syntax: "humanoid figure, wet grey-green sheen skin,
  overlapping shifting facial visages, frozen mid-stride stillness, lake
  water texture, dusk lighting, folk horror, unsettling"

ORIGINALITY VERIFICATION NOTE: Distinct from existing wet-haired ghost
archetypes through its freeze-when-observed mechanic and water-memory
origin logic rather than vengeful-spirit framing, and through overlapping
multi-visage design rather than a single fixed face.

14. ADVANCED VERSION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Add for franchise-scale or multi-form creature work:

{TRANSFORMATION_SEQUENCE_MAP} — if the creature evolves/transforms across
  the story, generate a locked token per transformation stage with explicit
  visual bridge notes between stages
{MULTI_INSTANCE_VARIATION} — rules for how multiple instances of the same
  creature type may vary (useful for "swarm" or "infection" horror) while
  keeping a recognizable shared identity
{PRACTICAL_EFFECS_FEASIBILITY_NOTE} — a note on which design elements would
  be achievable via practical effects vs. requiring VFX, for hybrid
  production planning (feeds Template 14)
{CROSS_MEDIA_CONSISTENCY_SET} — locked token variants optimized separately
  for still-image marketing materials vs. in-motion video generation

ADVANCED OUTPUT ADDITION: Request a "DRIFT-RESISTANT CORE TOKEN" — a
maximally compressed 8-10 word version of the consistency lock token
containing ONLY the 2-3 most drift-resistant, load-bearing visual details,
for use in tools/platforms with very short effective prompt-adherence
windows.
