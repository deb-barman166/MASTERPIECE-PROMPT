================================================================================
43 — MARKETING
================================================================================

TITLE
Marketing Strategy Master Template

PURPOSE
Develop marketing strategy grounded in a specific target customer and
measurable objective, rather than generic "brand awareness" tactics
disconnected from a clear business outcome.

IDEAL AI MODELS
Claude, GPT-4/5, Gemini

DIFFICULTY LEVEL
Intermediate → Expert

WHEN TO USE
- Channel strategy, campaign planning, positioning development,
  marketing budget allocation, messaging framework development

EXPECTED OUTPUT
A marketing strategy tied to a specific customer segment, measurable
objective, and channel selection justified by where that specific
customer actually spends attention.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUSINESS OBJECTIVE (measurable) → TARGET CUSTOMER (specific segment)
→ CUSTOMER'S ACTUAL ATTENTION LOCATION → MESSAGING (problem/solution
fit) → CHANNEL SELECTION (justified by customer location, not
popularity) → BUDGET ALLOCATION LOGIC → MEASUREMENT PLAN

WHY EACH SECTION EXISTS
- Measurable Objective first: marketing without a specific measurable
  goal (leads, signups, revenue — not just "awareness") can't be
  evaluated or improved; this must anchor every subsequent decision.
- Channel justified by customer location: choosing channels because
  they're popular/trendy rather than because the specific target
  customer actually spends attention there is a primary cause of wasted
  marketing spend.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VARIABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
{OBJECTIVE} {TARGET_CUSTOMER} {BUDGET} {TIME_HORIZON}

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INPUT STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Develop a marketing strategy to achieve {OBJECTIVE} (measurable target)
for {TARGET_CUSTOMER}. Budget: {BUDGET}, timeframe: {TIME_HORIZON}.
Identify where this specific customer segment actually spends attention
(not generic channel popularity). Develop core messaging addressing
their specific problem. Select and justify channels based on customer
location and budget fit. Propose a measurement plan tied directly back
to {OBJECTIVE}."

OUTPUT STRUCTURE
- Channel strategy with customer-location justification per channel
- Core messaging framework
- Measurement plan tied to the stated objective

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REASONING LAYER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Is the objective genuinely measurable, or is it a vague goal
   ("increase awareness") that can't be evaluated afterward?
2. Is each selected channel justified by evidence this specific
   customer segment is actually there, or by general channel popularity?
3. Does the budget allocation match the actual cost/scale of the
   selected channels realistically, or is it spread too thin to be
   effective anywhere?

SELF-CORRECTION LAYER
Replace any vague objective with a specific number/metric; challenge any
channel choice that isn't justified by specific customer-location
evidence; consolidate budget toward fewer channels if currently spread
too thin to achieve meaningful presence in any one.

REFLECTION LAYER
"If this campaign ran and hit its stated objective, would we actually
know it worked — is the measurement plan specific enough to prove or
disprove success?"

FACT VERIFICATION
Any channel/platform usage statistics for the target segment should be
verified against current data rather than assumed from general/outdated
knowledge, since platform demographics shift.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUALITY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Objective is specific and measurable
[ ] Target customer is a specific segment, not "everyone"
[ ] Each channel justified by customer-location evidence
[ ] Budget allocation matches realistic channel costs
[ ] Measurement plan directly tied to the objective

OPTIMIZATION TIPS
Concentrate budget in 1-2 well-justified channels rather than spreading
thin across many — depth of presence usually outperforms shallow
presence everywhere. Write messaging from the customer's specific
problem outward, not from the product's features inward.

COMMON MISTAKES
Vague, unmeasurable objectives; channel selection based on trendiness
rather than customer location; budget spread too thin across too many
channels; messaging that leads with features rather than the customer's
problem; no clear measurement plan tied back to the objective.

PROFESSIONAL TIPS
Validate channel assumptions with a small, cheap test before committing
significant budget — don't assume where the customer is, verify with a
low-cost pilot. Revisit the measurement plan weekly against actuals, not
just at campaign end, to catch and correct course early.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE INPUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Develop a marketing strategy to achieve 200 free-tier signups in 60 days
for a developer tool (AI agent monitoring dashboard) targeting solo
indie developers building with AI agent frameworks. Budget: $500,
timeframe: 60 days.

EXAMPLE OUTPUT
[Strategy: Objective is specific (200 signups/60 days) — good anchor.
Target customer (solo indie devs building AI agents) is a narrow,
technically-fluent segment that concentrates attention in specific
developer communities (relevant subreddits, Discord servers for AI/agent
frameworks, dev-focused X/Twitter, Hacker News) rather than broad social
platforms — channel selection should follow this, not general
popularity. With a $500 budget, this rules out paid ads at meaningful
scale for this audience (developer-tool CPCs are often too high for
$500 to move 200 signups); instead, budget is better spent on: (1) time-
efficient content — a genuinely useful technical blog post/tool solving
a real pain point this audience has, shared directly in 3-4 relevant
communities where self-promotion is accepted, (2) a small $200-300 spend
on targeted developer-newsletter sponsorship (often better ROI per
qualified signup than broad social ads for this niche audience) — cost/
signup estimate should be validated with the newsletter's own past
sponsor data before committing. Measurement: track signups by referral
source explicitly (UTM-tagged links per channel) so which specific
community/channel drove signups is known, not just total count —
enabling budget reallocation toward whatever's working by day 30 rather
than waiting until day 60.]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADVANCED VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Add A/B messaging test design across channels, cohort-based signup-
quality tracking (not just volume — are these signups converting to
active users), and a fallback: "If early data shows one channel
significantly outperforming, reallocate remaining budget toward it
before the full timeframe elapses rather than waiting for a fixed
review point."

EXPERT VERSION
Layer in full customer-acquisition funnel modeling (awareness → signup
→ activation, not just top-of-funnel signups), attribution-modeling
nuance for multi-touch customer journeys, and creative-testing framework
for message/channel-fit optimization.

ENTERPRISE VERSION
Add multi-channel budget-allocation modeling across a full marketing mix,
brand-consistency governance across campaigns, and formal marketing-
attribution/ROI reporting structure for executive stakeholders.

UNIVERSAL VERSION (model-agnostic core)
"Marketing strategy for [OBJECTIVE] targeting [TARGET_CUSTOMER], budget
[BUDGET], timeframe [TIME_HORIZON]. Channels justified by customer
location. Measurement tied to objective."
