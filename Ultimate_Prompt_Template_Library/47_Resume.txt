================================================================================
47 — RESUME
================================================================================

TITLE
Resume Writing Master Template

PURPOSE
Write resume bullet points that quantify impact and demonstrate
specific, verifiable achievement — rather than vague responsibility
descriptions that fail to differentiate a candidate from others who held
similar titles.

IDEAL AI MODELS
Claude, GPT-4/5, Gemini

DIFFICULTY LEVEL
Beginner → Intermediate

WHEN TO USE
- Resume writing/editing, bullet point strengthening, tailoring a resume
  to a specific role, portfolio project descriptions

EXPECTED OUTPUT
Resume content with quantified, specific achievement bullets (using the
action-context-result structure) tailored to the target role, avoiding
generic responsibility language.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE/EXPERIENCE → TARGET JOB (what this resume is tailored toward) →
RAW ACHIEVEMENTS (what actually happened, unpolished) → QUANTIFICATION
(numbers/scale/impact) → ACTION-CONTEXT-RESULT STRUCTURE → RELEVANCE
FILTER (what matters for the target role specifically) → KEYWORD
ALIGNMENT (for ATS systems, without keyword-stuffing)

WHY EACH SECTION EXISTS
- Raw Achievements before polishing: asking for polish before capturing
  the actual, specific facts of what happened produces generic-sounding
  bullets even when the underlying work was impressive — facts must
  come first.
- Quantification: "improved performance" is forgettable; "reduced page
  load time by 40%, from 3.2s to 1.9s" is specific and credible —
  numbers are what separate a strong resume from a generic one, and
  must be actively sought even when not obviously available.
- Action-Context-Result structure: this structure (what I did, in what
  situation, with what measurable outcome) is what makes a bullet
  demonstrate actual impact rather than just list a responsibility.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VARIABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
{ROLE} {TARGET_JOB} {RAW_ACHIEVEMENTS} {AVAILABLE_METRICS}

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INPUT STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Help me write resume bullets for my role as {ROLE}, tailoring toward a
{TARGET_JOB} application. Here's what I actually did (raw, unpolished):
{RAW_ACHIEVEMENTS}. Available metrics/numbers: {AVAILABLE_METRICS} (if
I don't have exact numbers, help me identify what I could reasonably
estimate or what alternative evidence of impact I could use instead).
Structure each bullet as action-context-result. Filter for what's most
relevant to {TARGET_JOB} specifically, not everything I did. Naturally
incorporate relevant keywords for this role without keyword-stuffing."

OUTPUT STRUCTURE
- Polished bullets in action-context-result structure
- Quantified where possible, with specific alternative evidence where
  exact numbers aren't available
- Filtered/prioritized for relevance to the target role

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REASONING LAYER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Does each bullet demonstrate a specific, quantified or otherwise
   concrete outcome, or does it just describe a responsibility/duty?
2. Is this the most relevant framing of this achievement for the
   SPECIFIC target job, or a generic description that could apply to
   any application?
3. If exact numbers aren't available, is there a reasonable, honest way
   to estimate scale/impact, or specific alternative evidence (before/
   after comparison, scope description) that still demonstrates impact
   without fabricating a false-precision number?

SELF-CORRECTION LAYER
Replace any responsibility-only bullet ("responsible for managing X")
with an action-context-result version demonstrating actual outcome;
push for a number/scale estimate on any currently-vague achievement
claim; re-prioritize bullet order/selection based on genuine relevance
to the specific target job rather than chronological/generic importance.

REFLECTION LAYER
"Would a hiring manager reading this bullet understand specifically what
I accomplished and how it mattered, or would it read as
interchangeable with any other candidate who held a similar title?"

FACT VERIFICATION
Never fabricate or inflate metrics — if an exact number isn't known,
use honest, reasonably-justified estimation language or qualitative
scope description rather than a false-precision invented statistic.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUALITY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Each bullet uses action-context-result structure
[ ] Quantified where genuinely possible, honestly estimated where not
[ ] No fabricated or inflated metrics
[ ] Filtered/prioritized for relevance to the specific target role
[ ] Keywords incorporated naturally, not stuffed
[ ] No generic responsibility-only descriptions remaining

OPTIMIZATION TIPS
Start every bullet with a strong action verb, varied across bullets (not
"managed" five times in a row). For achievements without an obvious
number, ask "compared to what would have happened otherwise" or "at what
scale" to surface a reasonable quantification angle. Tailor bullet
selection and emphasis per application rather than using one static
resume for every job.

COMMON MISTAKES
Responsibility-only bullets with no demonstrated outcome; vague
achievement claims without any quantification attempt; fabricated or
suspiciously round/inflated numbers; generic, non-tailored resume used
identically for every application; keyword-stuffing that reads
unnaturally.

PROFESSIONAL TIPS
Keep a running personal "achievement log" throughout each role
(specific numbers, scope, outcomes) as they happen — reconstructing
quantified impact months or years later from memory is far harder and
less accurate than capturing it in the moment. Tailor bullet emphasis
(not fabricated content, just selection/framing) specifically to each
target role's stated priorities.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE INPUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Help me write resume bullets for my role as a self-taught AI developer
(personal/portfolio projects, not formal employment), tailoring toward
an AI/ML internship application. Here's what I actually did: built a
RAG system combining vector and keyword search that performed better
than a pure-vector baseline; built a multi-agent system with 100+
coordinated agents using Google ADK. Available metrics: the RAG system's
accuracy improvement was measured in testing but I'd need to check exact
numbers; the multi-agent system doesn't have a clean metric, just the
scale/complexity itself.

EXAMPLE OUTPUT
[Bullets:

"Designed and built a hybrid dense/sparse retrieval system for RAG
pipelines, combining vector and keyword search with re-ranking;
benchmarked against a pure-vector baseline across multiple datasets to
validate the accuracy improvement before finalizing the architecture."
[Note: once you confirm the exact accuracy delta from your testing,
replace the general "validate the accuracy improvement" phrase with the
specific number — e.g. "...achieving a measured X% improvement in
retrieval accuracy over baseline" — specific numbers here will
meaningfully strengthen this bullet, so it's worth digging up your
actual test results before finalizing.]

"Architected a multi-agent orchestration system coordinating 100+
autonomous agents using Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK), designing
the communication/coordination layer to manage task delegation and
state across the full agent swarm." [Note: since there's no clean single
metric here, the scale itself (100+ coordinated agents) IS the
quantification — this is honest and still demonstrates significant
technical complexity without needing a fabricated metric.]

Both bullets lead with a strong action verb (Designed/Architected),
provide enough technical context for an ML-literate reader to understand
what was actually built, and are framed toward what an AI/ML internship
reviewer would specifically value (retrieval systems and agent
orchestration are both directly relevant technical skills for that
target role).]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADVANCED VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Add per-application tailoring workflow (how to adjust bullet emphasis/
selection across 3-5 different target-role variants from the same base
achievement set), and a fallback: "If a genuinely impressive achievement
has no available quantification and no reasonable estimation angle,
prioritize a specific qualitative scope/complexity description over
omitting the achievement or inventing a number."

EXPERT VERSION
Layer in full ATS (applicant tracking system) keyword-optimization
analysis against a specific job posting's actual language, competitive
positioning analysis (how this resume compares to a typical
competitive candidate pool for the target role), and narrative-
coherence review across the full resume (does the story it tells
support the target role convincingly as a whole, not just bullet-by-
bullet).

ENTERPRISE VERSION
(For career-services/recruiting use) Add batch resume-review rubric for
consistent feedback across many candidates, industry-specific
benchmark-language libraries, and formal resume-screening-criteria
alignment documentation.

UNIVERSAL VERSION (model-agnostic core)
"Help me write resume bullets for [ROLE], tailored toward [TARGET_JOB].
Raw achievements: [RAW_ACHIEVEMENTS]. Action-context-result structure,
honestly quantified, filtered for relevance to this specific role."
