================================================================================
20 — DOCUMENTARY
================================================================================

TITLE
AI Documentary-Style Video Master Template

PURPOSE
Direct AI video/narrative tools to produce documentary-style content
(observational footage look, authentic pacing) that avoids the overly
polished, "commercial" feel that undermines documentary credibility.

IDEAL AI MODELS
Sora, Runway Gen-3, Veo (footage generation), Claude/GPT (narrative
structure + narration script)

DIFFICULTY LEVEL
Expert

WHEN TO USE
- Documentary-style B-roll, mockumentary concepts, historical
  reenactment previsualization, observational-style brand storytelling,
  educational documentary segments

EXPECTED OUTPUT
Footage or a script/structure with an authentic, observational quality —
naturalistic camera behavior, unposed-feeling moments, and a narrative
arc grounded in real information rather than pure spectacle.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUBJECT/TOPIC
  ↓
DOCUMENTARY ANGLE (the specific angle/thesis, not just the topic)
  ↓
NARRATIVE STRUCTURE (chronological / thematic / character-driven)
  ↓
FOOTAGE STYLE (observational handheld / static interview / archival-style)
  ↓
CAMERA BEHAVIOR (naturalistic, not cinematically composed)
  ↓
NARRATION/VOICE APPROACH (narrator / interview-driven / text-on-screen)
  ↓
FACTUAL GROUNDING (what real information anchors this)
  ↓
EMOTIONAL THROUGHLINE
  ↓
PACING (deliberate, allows moments to breathe)

WHY EACH SECTION EXISTS
- Documentary Angle: "a documentary about coffee" isn't a thesis; "how
  climate change is forcing coffee farmers to relocate their entire
  livelihoods" is — documentaries succeed on a specific, arguable angle,
  not a broad topic.
- Camera Behavior (naturalistic): AI video models default to smooth,
  cinematic camera movement, which paradoxically reads as less authentic
  for documentary purposes — slight handheld imperfection or static
  observational framing often serves the genre better.
- Factual Grounding: even AI-assisted concept work for documentary
  content must be traceable to real information; documentary is a
  truth-claiming genre, and fabricated "facts" presented in this style
  are actively misleading.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VARIABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
{TOPIC} — the subject matter
{ANGLE} — the specific thesis/perspective
{STRUCTURE} — chronological / thematic / character-driven
{FOOTAGE_STYLE} — observational handheld / static interview / archival
{NARRATION_APPROACH} — voiceover narrator / interview-led / on-screen text
{FACTUAL_SOURCE} — what grounds the content in reality
{TONE} — investigative / intimate / urgent / contemplative

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INPUT STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Develop a documentary-style piece about {TOPIC}, with the specific
angle: '{ANGLE}'. Structure: {STRUCTURE}. Footage style: {FOOTAGE_STYLE},
with naturalistic, observational camera behavior (avoid overly smooth/
cinematic movement). Narration approach: {NARRATION_APPROACH}. Ground
the narrative in real, verifiable information from {FACTUAL_SOURCE}.
Tone: {TONE}. Allow pacing to breathe — avoid rapid-cut commercial
pacing."

OUTPUT STRUCTURE
- Narrative structure/script with documentary pacing and factual anchor
- (If generating footage) naturalistic, observational-style clips

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REASONING LAYER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Is the angle specific and arguable, or is it just a restatement of
   the general topic?
2. Does the footage/narrative style read as observational and authentic,
   or does it accidentally carry commercial/advertising visual language?
3. Is every factual claim in the narrative traceable to a real,
   verifiable source, especially if this content will be presented as
   factual/informational?

SELF-CORRECTION LAYER
If the piece feels generic or commercial rather than documentary:
- Sharpen the angle into a specific, debatable thesis statement
- Slow the pacing and reduce cinematic camera polish toward more
  observational, static, or naturalistically imperfect framing
- Re-verify every stated fact against a real source, removing or
  flagging anything unconfirmed

REFLECTION LAYER
"Does this feel like it's showing me something true and specific, or
does it feel like it's selling me something?"

FACT VERIFICATION
This is the most critical layer for this template category: every
factual claim, statistic, date, or quote must be verified against
reliable sources. If a claim cannot be verified, it must be explicitly
flagged as unconfirmed or removed — documentary-style content carries an
implicit truth claim that fictional content does not.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUALITY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Angle is specific and arguable, not just the general topic
[ ] Footage/narrative style reads as observational, not commercial
[ ] Camera behavior is naturalistic, not overly polished
[ ] Every factual claim is verified or explicitly flagged as unconfirmed
[ ] Pacing allows moments to breathe rather than rapid-cutting
[ ] Emotional throughline is grounded in real human stakes, not
    manufactured drama

OPTIMIZATION TIPS
- Write the thesis/angle as a single arguable sentence before any
  visual or structural planning begins.
- Study real documentary pacing (long takes, silence, unresolved
  moments) rather than defaulting to trailer-style rapid editing.
- When using AI-generated footage for documentary-adjacent work, always
  disclose that footage is AI-generated/illustrative rather than actual
  documentary recording, to maintain audience trust.

COMMON MISTAKES
- Broad topic treated as if it were a specific angle
- Overly polished, cinematic camera movement undermining authenticity
- Fabricated or unverified "facts" presented with documentary authority
- Rapid, commercial-style pacing that doesn't match the genre's
  contemplative conventions

PROFESSIONAL TIPS
- Real documentary filmmaking is built on research first, footage
  second — replicate this by doing thorough source-verification before
  any generation/scripting begins.
- Use silence and stillness deliberately; documentary's emotional power
  often comes from what's NOT rushed past.
- Always maintain a clear, honest boundary between "documentary-style"
  (illustrative/AI-generated) and actual documentary (real recorded
  footage/verified reporting) when presenting finished work to any
  audience.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE INPUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Develop a documentary-style piece about the rise of self-taught
developers building production AI systems without formal CS degrees,
with the specific angle: 'access to modern AI tools has compressed a
decade-long learning curve into 1-2 years for highly motivated
self-learners, fundamentally changing who gets to build serious
software.' Structure: character-driven, following one representative
case study in depth rather than surveying many. Footage style:
observational handheld, following the subject in their real working
environment. Narration approach: minimal voiceover, mostly interview-led
with on-screen text for key statistics. Ground the narrative in real,
verifiable information from industry hiring/education trend reports.
Tone: intimate, contemplative, quietly optimistic.

EXAMPLE OUTPUT
[Structure outline: Cold open — close, handheld shot of hands typing,
no context yet, subject's voice begins mid-thought about a specific
technical problem they solved. Act 1 establishes who they are and their
environment naturalistically (their actual desk, their actual tools, no
staged "hero shots"). Act 2 follows the specific arc of one real project
they built, intercut with brief on-screen text citing verified statistics
on the growth of self-directed technical learning. Act 3 sits with a
quieter, unresolved reflection on what this access means for the future
of who builds software — avoiding a neat, falsely triumphant conclusion.
Note: any statistic used must be sourced from a specific named,
verifiable report before inclusion, not an AI-generated approximation.]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADVANCED VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Add: multi-source triangulation requirement (any significant factual
claim must be corroborated across at least two independent real
sources), archival-footage-style integration notes (how to visually
distinguish AI-generated "archival-style" material from real archival
footage, maintaining audience honesty), and a fallback: "If a compelling
narrative moment relies on an unverifiable claim, restructure around a
verified adjacent fact rather than including the unverified version."

EXPERT VERSION
Layer in: full ethical-documentary-practice checklist (informed consent
considerations for any real people depicted or discussed, fair
representation of differing perspectives on contested topics), sound-
design notes for observational authenticity (ambient/diegetic sound
prioritized over score), and a formal fact-checking log structure
documenting the source for every factual claim in the piece.

ENTERPRISE VERSION
Add: legal/editorial review requirements for factual content
(defamation risk review, fair-use/rights clearance for any real
footage/quotes used), platform content-authenticity labeling compliance
(disclosure requirements for AI-generated documentary-style content on
major platforms), and a formal editorial-standards document for
newsroom/production-company use.

UNIVERSAL VERSION (model-agnostic core)
"A documentary-style piece on [TOPIC], angle: '[ANGLE]'. [STRUCTURE]
structure, [FOOTAGE_STYLE] style, [NARRATION_APPROACH] narration,
grounded in verified facts from [FACTUAL_SOURCE], [TONE] tone."
This is primarily a reasoning/scripting template (Claude/GPT); any
resulting footage generation via Sora/Runway/Veo must be clearly
disclosed as AI-generated/illustrative rather than actual documentary
recording.
