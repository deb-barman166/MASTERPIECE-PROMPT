56 — FINANCE
================================================================================

TITLE
Personal/Business Finance Analysis Master Template

PURPOSE
Get clear, factual financial analysis and educational explanation from
AI assistants — helping understand concepts, calculations, and tradeoffs
so the requester can make their own informed decision, rather than
receiving confident personalized financial advice that AI is not
qualified to give.

IDEAL AI MODELS
Claude, GPT-4/5, Gemini

DIFFICULTY LEVEL
Beginner → Advanced

WHEN TO USE
- Understanding financial concepts, budget analysis, comparing financial
  options factually (loan terms, investment vehicle mechanics), business
  financial modeling, calculation assistance

EXPECTED OUTPUT
Clear factual information and calculations that help the requester
understand their options and make their own decision — explicitly not
personalized financial advice or specific recommendations to buy/sell/
invest.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FINANCIAL QUESTION/SITUATION → RELEVANT FACTS (numbers, terms, context)
→ CONCEPT EXPLANATION NEEDED → CALCULATION (if applicable, shown with
methodology) → OPTIONS COMPARISON (factual, not a recommendation) →
KEY TRADEOFFS TO CONSIDER → ADVISOR DISCLAIMER (not a substitute for a
licensed financial professional for personalized advice)

WHY EACH SECTION EXISTS
- Factual, not advisory framing: financial decisions have real,
  significant consequences and depend on personal circumstances (risk
  tolerance, full financial picture, tax situation, goals) that an AI
  assistant cannot fully know or is not qualified to weigh — the
  template must produce educational/factual output the requester uses
  to inform their own decision, not a confident recommendation.
- Calculation methodology shown: for any numeric calculation (compound
  interest, loan amortization, ROI), showing the actual methodology
  (not just a final number) lets the requester verify the math and
  understand it, rather than blindly trusting an opaque figure.
- Key Tradeoffs (not a recommendation): presenting the genuine tradeoffs
  of different options lets the requester weigh them against their own
  full situation, which the AI doesn't have complete visibility into.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VARIABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
{FINANCIAL_QUESTION} {RELEVANT_NUMBERS} {OPTIONS_TO_COMPARE}

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INPUT STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Help me understand: {FINANCIAL_QUESTION}. Relevant numbers/context:
{RELEVANT_NUMBERS}. Explain the underlying concept clearly. If
calculation is needed, show the methodology explicitly so I can verify
it. If comparing options ({OPTIONS_TO_COMPARE}), lay out the factual
tradeoffs of each rather than recommending one — help me understand what
to consider given my own situation and goals, since you don't have my
complete financial picture."

OUTPUT STRUCTURE
- Clear concept explanation
- Calculation with visible methodology (if applicable)
- Factual tradeoff comparison, not a specific recommendation
- Note that this is educational information, not personalized advice

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REASONING LAYER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Is this response giving the requester the factual information/
   calculation they need to decide for themselves, or is it drifting
   into a confident personalized recommendation ("you should do X")
   that depends on their full financial picture the AI doesn't have?
2. If a calculation is involved, is the methodology shown clearly enough
   for the requester to verify or adapt it to slightly different
   numbers themselves?
3. Are the tradeoffs presented genuinely balanced/factual, or does the
   framing subtly favor one option in a way that resembles advice rather
   than neutral information?

SELF-CORRECTION LAYER
Reframe any response that has drifted into "you should..." recommendation
language into factual "here's what this option means, here's what that
one means" framing; add explicit calculation methodology if a number was
given without showing the work; add a note pointing toward a licensed
financial professional for genuinely personalized decisions with
significant stakes.

REFLECTION LAYER
"Is this response helping the requester become better informed to make
their own decision, or is it making the decision for them in a way that
depends on personal context I don't actually have?"

FACT VERIFICATION
Any cited rates, rules, or figures (tax brackets, interest rate
context, regulatory rules) should be verified against current, reliable
sources, since financial rules/rates change and outdated information
could lead to a costly mistake.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUALITY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Concept explained clearly and accurately
[ ] Calculation methodology shown explicitly, not just a final number
[ ] Options compared factually, not steered toward one recommendation
[ ] No confident personalized advice presented as if it accounts for the
    requester's full situation
[ ] Note included that this isn't a substitute for licensed professional
    advice for high-stakes personalized decisions

OPTIMIZATION TIPS
Provide as much relevant factual context as possible (actual numbers,
terms, timeframes) for the most useful, concrete explanation. Ask
explicitly for the calculation methodology to be shown if you want to
verify or adapt it yourself. For genuinely high-stakes decisions
(significant investments, major loans), use AI to build understanding
before/alongside consulting a licensed professional, not as a
replacement for one.

COMMON MISTAKES
Treating AI financial explanation as personalized advice accounting for
one's full situation; not verifying current rates/rules that may have
changed; accepting a calculation's output without understanding or
checking the methodology; using AI as a sole source for high-stakes
financial decisions without professional consultation.

PROFESSIONAL TIPS
Use AI most effectively for building genuine understanding of concepts
and running "what if" calculations across scenarios, which strengthens
your own decision-making capability. For any decision with significant
financial stakes or complex personal circumstances (tax situations,
estate planning, significant investment decisions), consult a licensed
financial advisor or tax professional who can account for your complete
situation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE INPUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Help me understand: how does compound interest actually work, and if I
save a fixed amount monthly, roughly how does starting 5 years earlier
affect the outcome? Relevant numbers: $200/month, hypothetical 7% annual
return, comparing starting now vs. 5 years from now, both running to age
60.

EXAMPLE OUTPUT
[Concept explanation: Compound interest means you earn returns not just
on your original contributions, but on previously-earned returns too —
so growth accelerates over time rather than staying linear, which is
why TIME in the market matters more than most people initially expect
relative to the monthly amount itself.

CALCULATION (methodology shown, using the future value of an annuity
formula, monthly contributions, monthly compounding at 7%/year ≈
0.5833%/month):

Scenario A — start now, contribute $200/month for (let's say) 35 years
to age 60: [actual computed result via code execution showing the
formula applied]

Scenario B — start 5 years later, contribute $200/month for 30 years to
the same age 60: [actual computed result]

[Note: exact numbers depend on your specific current age — the
calculation above assumes illustrative years; provide your actual
current age for a precise version]

The key factual takeaway from the methodology: the DIFFERENCE between
these two scenarios comes disproportionately from the growth on the
earliest contributions compounding over the longest additional stretch
of time — this is why the 5-year head start typically matters more in
percentage terms than the raw $12,000 (5 years × $200/mo × 12) difference
in total contributions alone would suggest.

This is a factual illustration of how compound interest and timing
interact, not a recommendation about your specific savings strategy —
your actual best approach depends on your full financial picture (other
debts, emergency fund status, actual expected returns, goals), which is
worth discussing with a financial advisor for a personalized plan,
especially before committing to a specific long-term strategy.]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADVANCED VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Add sensitivity analysis across a range of assumed return rates (since
7% is illustrative, not guaranteed) showing how the conclusion holds or
changes across a realistic range, and a fallback: "If a requested
calculation depends heavily on an uncertain assumption (future market
returns, inflation), explicitly show results across a reasonable range
rather than a single point estimate that implies false precision."

EXPERT VERSION
Layer in tax-treatment factual explanation (how different account types
are taxed differently, factually, without recommending a specific
account choice), and inflation-adjusted ("real") return illustration
alongside nominal figures for a more complete factual picture.

ENTERPRISE VERSION
(For fintech/educational content platforms) Add regulatory-compliance
review requirements for any financial content/calculators presented to
end users, clear and prominent disclaimer language meeting relevant
regulatory standards, and factual-accuracy review workflow for any
published financial educational content.

UNIVERSAL VERSION (model-agnostic core)
"Help me understand [FINANCIAL_QUESTION] with these numbers:
[RELEVANT_NUMBERS]. Explain the concept, show calculation methodology,
compare options factually without recommending one — I'll decide based
on my own full situation."
