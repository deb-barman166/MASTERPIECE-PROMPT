================================================================================
04 — THUMBNAIL GENERATOR
================================================================================

TITLE
YouTube/Video Thumbnail Master Template

PURPOSE
Generate high-click-through-rate thumbnails that communicate the video's
value proposition in under 1 second of viewing, optimized for small mobile
display sizes.

IDEAL AI MODELS
Midjourney v6+, Ideogram v2 (text rendering), DALL-E 3, Photoshop
Generative Fill (for compositing with real face photos)

DIFFICULTY LEVEL
Beginner → Intermediate

WHEN TO USE
- YouTube videos, course platform thumbnails, podcast video covers,
  webinar promo images, video ad thumbnails

EXPECTED OUTPUT
A thumbnail (1280x720 standard) with high contrast, a clear focal point,
and (optionally) legible short text, readable at 120px mobile width.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VIDEO TOPIC / HOOK
  ↓
FOCAL SUBJECT (face/object/graphic)
  ↓
EMOTIONAL EXPRESSION (if face-based)
  ↓
BACKGROUND SIMPLIFICATION (contrast against subject)
  ↓
TEXT OVERLAY (if any — max 4 words)
  ↓
COLOR CONTRAST STRATEGY
  ↓
COMPOSITION (rule of thirds, subject placement)
  ↓
PLATFORM SPEC (1280x720, safe zone for title/duration overlay)

WHY EACH SECTION EXISTS
- Emotional Expression: thumbnails with clear human emotion (shock,
  curiosity, joy) consistently outperform neutral expressions — this
  isn't optional polish, it's the primary CTR driver.
- Background Simplification: cluttered backgrounds reduce thumbnail
  legibility at small size; the subject must pop immediately.
- Text Overlay limit (4 words): anything longer becomes unreadable at
  mobile thumbnail size and competes with the platform's own title text.
- Platform Spec safe zone: YouTube overlays duration/progress-bar
  elements in the bottom-right — critical content shouldn't sit there.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VARIABLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
{VIDEO_TOPIC} — what the video is about
{HOOK} — the curiosity gap or value prop to visually imply
{FOCAL_SUBJECT} — face / product / graphic that anchors the frame
{EMOTION} — shocked / excited / curious / confident / concerned
{TEXT_OVERLAY} — max 4 words, if used
{ACCENT_COLOR} — high-contrast color for text/highlight elements
{BACKGROUND_STYLE} — simplified/blurred vs. contextual

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INPUT STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Create a YouTube thumbnail for a video about {VIDEO_TOPIC}, implying the
hook: {HOOK}. Focal subject: {FOCAL_SUBJECT} with a {EMOTION} expression,
positioned off-center per rule of thirds. Background: {BACKGROUND_STYLE},
simplified to keep focus on subject. Text overlay (large, bold, max 4
words): '{TEXT_OVERLAY}' in {ACCENT_COLOR}. High contrast, saturated
colors, 1280x720, keep bottom-right corner clear for duration overlay."

OUTPUT STRUCTURE
- 1280x720 image, high contrast, subject clearly separated from background
- Text (if any) legible at 120px width simulation

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REASONING LAYER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Does this thumbnail create a curiosity gap without being clickbait-
   deceptive (i.e., does the video actually deliver on the implied hook)?
2. Is the focal point identifiable within 0.5 seconds at thumbnail size?
3. Does the color palette stand out against a white YouTube UI background
   and against typical competing thumbnails in this niche?

SELF-CORRECTION LAYER
If the concept feels flat:
- Increase emotional intensity of the expression by one notch
- Simplify the background further (blur increase or solid color swap)
- Reduce text to 2-3 words maximum, relying more on visual hook

REFLECTION LAYER
"Shrink this mentally to a postage-stamp size — is the single most
important element still the first thing I notice?"

FACT VERIFICATION
If the thumbnail implies a specific claim/number/result, ensure it
matches the actual video content to avoid misleading-thumbnail platform
penalties.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUALITY CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Clear single focal point
[ ] High contrast between subject and background
[ ] Text (if any) is 4 words or fewer, bold, high-contrast
[ ] Emotion/expression is clear and intentional
[ ] Bottom-right corner kept clear
[ ] Legible at small mobile size

OPTIMIZATION TIPS
- Test thumbnail against a shrunk 120px-wide version before finalizing.
- Use complementary color pairs (blue/orange, purple/yellow) for
  maximum perceptual contrast.
- Reserve pure clickbait exaggeration for genuinely high-stakes hooks;
  moderate curiosity gaps sustain channel trust better long-term.

COMMON MISTAKES
- Cramming multiple faces/objects competing for attention
- Using small, thin fonts that disappear at thumbnail size
- Matching background color to subject color, killing contrast
- Overpromising visually relative to actual video content

PROFESSIONAL TIPS
- Maintain a swipe file of high-performing thumbnails in your niche
  (not to copy, but to identify current visual-language patterns).
- A/B test 2-3 thumbnail concepts per video if the platform supports it.
- Keep a consistent visual "signature" (font, color treatment, framing
  style) across a channel's thumbnails to build recognizability.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE INPUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Create a YouTube thumbnail for a video about "why your Python code runs
slow," implying the hook: one common mistake is costing 10x performance.
Focal subject: a young developer with a shocked/realizing expression,
positioned off-center per rule of thirds. Background: blurred code editor
in dark theme, simplified to keep focus on subject. Text overlay (large,
bold, max 4 words): '10x SLOWER?!' in bright yellow. High contrast,
saturated colors, 1280x720, keep bottom-right corner clear for duration
overlay.

EXAMPLE OUTPUT
[Image: A developer in the left third of the frame, eyes wide, mouth
slightly open in realization, lit with a cool blue key light. Right two-
thirds shows a heavily blurred dark-theme code editor for context without
distraction. Bold yellow text "10x SLOWER?!" sits in the upper-right,
clear of the bottom-right corner. Strong blue/yellow color contrast.]

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADVANCED VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Add: multi-thumbnail variant generation instructions (produce 3 distinct
emotional/compositional approaches to A/B test), explicit brand-
consistency constraints (recurring font/color treatment across a channel),
and a fallback: "If the AI-generated face looks uncanny, generate the
background/graphic elements only and composite a real photo separately."

EXPERT VERSION
Layer in: CTR-psychology annotations per element (why this specific
emotion/color/framing choice statistically correlates with higher CTR in
this content category), platform-specific safe-zone maps (YouTube vs.
TikTok vs. Instagram Reels cover differ), and thumbnail-title pairing
logic (ensuring the thumbnail and title work together as one cohesive
hook, not redundant or contradictory messages).

ENTERPRISE VERSION
Add: channel-wide thumbnail template system (consistent structure across
50+ videos with swappable subject/text), brand-guideline compliance
(exact font files, color hex values, logo placement rules), and a
performance-feedback loop instruction: "Incorporate CTR data from the
previous 10 videos to bias style choices toward historically higher-
performing patterns."

UNIVERSAL VERSION (model-agnostic core)
"A high-contrast YouTube thumbnail: [FOCAL_SUBJECT] with [EMOTION]
expression, simplified [BACKGROUND_STYLE] background, bold text
'[TEXT_OVERLAY]' in [ACCENT_COLOR], 1280x720."
Works across all major image models; route text-heavy versions to
Ideogram specifically, since most other models render thumbnail text
illegibly or with spelling errors.
