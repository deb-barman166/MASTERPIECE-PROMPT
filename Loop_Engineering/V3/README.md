# Loop Engineering Knowledge Library

> A comprehensive, developer-friendly knowledge base covering every aspect of **Loop Engineering** — the discipline of designing, building, optimizing, and managing iterative AI/LLM workflows.

---

## What is Loop Engineering?

Loop Engineering is the practice of building systems where AI iterates through cycles of **reasoning, acting, observing, and refining** until a goal is achieved. Instead of single-pass prompts, loop-engineered systems think step-by-step, use tools, self-correct, and adapt — making them capable of solving complex, real-world tasks reliably.
